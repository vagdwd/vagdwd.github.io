Engineering Competency Matrix: L1 to L4 Growth Framework

Vageesh Dwivedi • April 4, 2022 • 6 min read

A transparent competency matrix demystifies career progression, aligning organizational expectations with an engineer's personal aspirations. Across high-performance engineering organizations, we calibrate growth across 4 fundamental competency axes from Level 1 (Junior/Mid) to Level 4 (Staff/Principal):

1. Continuous Learning & Specialization

  • L1: Gains expertise in core language and domain; displays intellectual curiosity; receptive to peer feedback.
  • L2: Mentors and coaches junior developers; shares post-mortems and best practices; acts as an effective technical interviewer.
  • L3: Builds deep expertise through experimentation; sponsors architectural innovation; provides technical supervision for multi-system challenges.
  • L4: Provides org-wide strategic technical guidance; leads multi-product architecture frameworks; recognized industry authority who stays abreast of competitor developments.

2. Execution Discipline

  • L1: Demonstrates commitment, ownership, and zero-defect focus on assigned tasks.
  • L2: Operates independently without constant supervision; takes tactical decisions and adapts when requirements shift.
  • L3: Uses metrics, code reviews, and telemetry to cultivate high execution discipline across their pod.
  • L4: Sustains execution discipline in highly ambiguous, high-risk, dynamic environments across cross-functional dependencies.

3. Influence & Communication

  • L1: Clear, confident written and verbal communication in pull requests and team standups.
  • L2: Fine-tunes communication depending on audience (1-on-1s, sprint reviews, stakeholder demos).
  • L3: Exercises cross-functional influence without authority, driving consensus across design, product, and QA.
  • L4: Boundaryless leadership; builds executive partnerships and strategic alignment across department boundaries.

4. Initiative & Proactive Ownership

  • L1: Acts now to minimize potential defects; calls out blockers promptly.
  • L2: Identifies recurring patterns and implements preventive tooling to solve problems for the future.
  • L3: Identifies and resolves challenges that extend well beyond their team's immediate purview.
  • L4: Leads large-scale organizational change initiatives; models resilience and healthy risk-taking.
